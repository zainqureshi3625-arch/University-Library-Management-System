# Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RACI)

Project: University Library Management System

## 1. RACI Definitions

- **R (Responsible):** The person who does the actual work.
- **A (Accountable):** The person who is answerable for completing the task. Every task has exactly one A.
- **A/R:** The same person is accountable and does the work.
- **C (Consulted):** The person who gives advice or suggestions.
- **I (Informed):** The person who is told about the completed work.
