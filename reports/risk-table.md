# Risk Management Table

Project: University Library Management System

## 1. How to Rate a Risk

- **Likelihood:** how likely the risk is to happen (Low, Medium or High).
- **Impact:** how big the effect is if it happens (Low, Medium or High).
- **Risk Level:** found by combining likelihood and impact. High means act first, Medium means watch it, Low means keep an eye on it.
