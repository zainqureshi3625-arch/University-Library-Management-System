# Stakeholder Analysis

University Library Management System

## 1. What is a Stakeholder?

A stakeholder is a person or group who uses the library system or is affected by it. This document lists the stakeholders and what each of them needs from the system.

## 2. Stakeholders

| No. | Stakeholder | Role in the library | Importance |
|:---:|---|---|:---:|
| 1 | Student | Searches for, borrows and returns books | High |
| 2 | Librarian | Manages books, members, borrowing and returns | High |
| 3 | Faculty Member | Borrows books for teaching and research | Medium |
| 4 | Library Administrator | Controls library rules, accounts and reports | High |
| 5 | University Management | Decides about library budget and services | Low |
| 6 | IT Support Staff | Installs, backs up and looks after the system | Medium |

## 3. Stakeholder Needs

| Need ID | Stakeholder | Need | Why it matters |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| SN-01 | Student | Search for books and see if a book is available | Saves time and avoids a wasted trip to the library |
| SN-02 | Student | Reserve a book that is already borrowed | Gets the book when it is returned |
| SN-03 | Student | See my borrowed books and their due dates | Helps me return books on time |
| SN-04 | Student | Get a reminder before the due date | Avoids late returns and penalties |
| SN-05 | Librarian | Add, update and remove book and book copy records | Keeps the library records correct |
| SN-06 | Librarian | Issue books to members and record returns quickly | Replaces the paper register and saves time |
| SN-07 | Librarian | See which books are overdue and send overdue notices | Gets the books back on time |
| SN-08 | Librarian | Manage book reservations | Gives books to members in a fair order |
| SN-09 | Faculty Member | Search for and borrow books for teaching and research | Books are needed for classes and research |
| SN-10 | Faculty Member | Reserve books that are needed for a course | Makes sure the books are ready when needed |
| SN-11 | Faculty Member | Get reminders for due and overdue books | Avoids keeping books too long |
| SN-12 | Library Administrator | Manage member accounts and library rules (loan period, number of books) | Different members need different rules |
| SN-13 | Library Administrator | See reports about borrowing and overdue books | Helps to plan and improve the library |
| SN-14 | Library Administrator | Control which user can do which task | Keeps library data safe |
| SN-15 | University Management | Get summary reports about library use | Helps to take decisions about budget and services |
| SN-16 | IT Support Staff | A system that is easy to install, back up and maintain | Less problems and less work |
| SN-17 | IT Support Staff | Safe storage of member and book data | Protects the privacy of members |

## 4. Notes

- There are 6 stakeholders and 17 needs.
- Importance shows how much the stakeholder affects the success of the project (High, Medium or Low).
- These needs will be used later to write the requirements and the user stories.
